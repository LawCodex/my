# TUNKU ABDUL RAHMAN PUTRA AL-HAJ PENSION ACT 1971

BE IT ENACTED by the Seri Paduka Baginda Yang di-Pertuan Agong with the advice and consent of the Dewan Negara and Dewan Rakyat in Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:

## 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Tunku Abdul Rahman Putra AlHaj Pension Act 1971.

## 2. Act 237

Notwithstanding the Members of Parliament (Remuneration) Act 1980 [Act 237] or any other law pertaining to the pensions and gratuities of Members of the Administration, Y.T.M. Tunku Abdul Rahman Putra Al-Haj shall, on his retirement from the office of Prime Minister of Malaysia, be entitled to the following pension and privileges:

(a) a tax free gratuity of RM200,000;

(b) a pension of four thousand ringgit shall be payable monthly on the first day of each month;

(c) a furnished residence shall maintained free of charge;

## 3. Pension and privileges under this Act additional to any allowance or privileges, if any

The pension and privileges set out in section 2 shall be additional to any allowance or other privileges due to him under any law relating to remuneration of Members of Parliament or the Legislative Assembly of any State.

## 4. Payment out of Consolidated Fund

Any expenditure incurred under section 2 shall be paid out of the Consolidated Fund.
