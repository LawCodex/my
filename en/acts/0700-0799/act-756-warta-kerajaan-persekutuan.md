PEMBERITAHUAN BERHUBUNG DENGAN AKTA PERUBATAN TRADISIONAL DAN

KOMPLEMENTARI 2013 [AKTA 756]

Diberitahu bahawa Akta Perubatan Tradisional dan Komplementari 2013 [Akta 756] tidak mempunyai memenuhi apa-apa kuat Fasal (3) kehendak kuasa Perkara undang-undang 66 kerana Perlembagaan tidak Persekutuan. Lihat Akta Perubatan Tradisional dan Komplementari 2016 [Akta 775].

[PN(U2)2758/III]

NOTIFICATION IN RELATION TO THE TRADITIONAL AND COMPLEMENTARY

MEDICINE ACT 2013 [ACT 756]

This is to notify that the Traditional and Complementary Medicine Act 2013 [Act 756] has Clause no force (3) of law for not fulfilling Article 66 of the Federal the Constitution. Traditional and Complementary Medicine Act 2016 [Act 775].
