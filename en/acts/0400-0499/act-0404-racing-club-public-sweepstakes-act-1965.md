# RACING CLUB (PUBLIC SWEEPSTAKES) ACT 1965

BE IT ENACTED by the Seri Paduka Baginda Yang di-Pertuan Agong with the advice and consent of the Dewan Negara and Dewan Rakyat in Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:

## 1. Short title and application

(1) This Act may be cited as the Racing Club (Public Sweepstakes) Act 1965.

(2) This Act shall apply throughout Malaysia.

## 2. Racing Clubs may promote public sweepstakes

(1) A racing club established in Malaysia may, after the commencement of this Act, promote a sweeptake in which any person (including a person not being a member of the club) may take part; and the *Betting Ordinance 1953 [Ord. 47 of 1953], shall not apply to any sweepstake so promoted.

(2) In this section—

“racing club” means a racing club which is a member of the Malayan Racing Association or a successor thereof; and

“sweepstakes” means any sweepstake promoted on the result of a horse race.

## 3.

(Omitted). *NOTE—The Betting Ordinance 1953 [Ord. 47 of 1953] has been revised as the Betting Act 1953

[Act 495].
