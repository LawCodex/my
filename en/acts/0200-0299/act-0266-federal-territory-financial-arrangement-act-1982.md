# FEDERAL TERRITORY (FINANCIAL ARRANGEMENT) ACT 1982

WHEREAS such financial arrangement has been agreed to between the Federation and the State of Selangor; NOW THEREFORE BE IT ENACTED by the Seri Paduka Baginda Yang di-Pertuan Agong with the advice and consent of the Dewan Negara and Dewan Rakyat in Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:

## 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Federal Territory (Financial Arrangement) Act 1982.

## 2. Payments made under financial arrangement

(1) The Federation shall pay to the State of Selangor all payments due to be made pursuant to the financial arrangement agreed to between the Federation and the State of Selangor in respect of the establishment of the Federal Territory.

(2) Any payment made by the Federation to the State of Selangor relating to the establishment of the Federal Territory before the date of the coming into force of this Act shall be deemed to have been lawfully appropriated and made under this Act.

## 3. Charge on the Consolidated Fund

All payments due to be made under subsection 2(1) shall be charged on the Consolidated Fund.
