# STATE GRANTS (MAINTENANCE OF LOCAL AUTHORITIES) ACT 1981

WHEREAS by Clause (3) of Article 109 of the Federal Constitution it is provided that Parliament may by law make grants for specific purposes to any of the States on such terms and conditions as may be provided by such law; NOW THEREFORE BE IT ENACTED by the Seri Paduka Baginda Yang di-Pertuan Agong with the advice and consent of the Dewan Negara and Dewan Rakyat in Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:

## 1. Short title and commencement

This Act may be cited as the State Grants (Maintenance of Local Authorities) Act 1981 and shall be deemed to have come into force on 1 January 1978.

## 2. Grant to any State by the Federation

(1) The Federation may from time to time make a grant to anyState for the maintenance of local authorities in that State.

(2) The amount of the grant made under subsection (1) shall be determined by the Minister responsible for local government after consultation with the Minister of Finance.

## 3. Federation may impose terms and conditions

The Federation, in making such grant, may impose terms and conditions to be observed by the State including a condition that the grant is to be spent by the local authorities for a specific purpose.
